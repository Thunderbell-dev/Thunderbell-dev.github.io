---

## Mainpage

**Purpose:** Introduction to the family and its activities.  
**Content:** Brief introduction to the family, main activities, links to other areas of the website.

## Guides

**Purpose:** Instructions and tips for members and interested parties.  
**Content:** Game mechanics, strategies for specific content, specific rules.

## Art Gallery

**Purpose:** Platform for creative contributions from members.  
**Content:** Fan art or other artistic works.
**Additional:** "Watermark Script"

## News

**Purpose:** Overview of upcoming and past events of the guild.  
**Content:** Dates, descriptions of the events, results or reports.
**Additional:** Raid schedule for repeat activities or when there are double box or gold events. Possibility of a dynamic schedule that can be displayed in different time zones.

## Progress Tracking

### Family Progress

**Purpose:** Tracking the achievements achieved.  
**Content:** List of achievements achieved.

### Hall of Fame

**Purpose:** Highlighting notable achievements and goals.  
**Content:** Examples like "X achieved DH+15 Demon Wings" or "Y achieved ZZZ action points".
