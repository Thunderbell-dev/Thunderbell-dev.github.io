Requirements

Use AOE skills for maximum efficiency

Bring 2 :Distance: or :Magic: pets for optimal damage

Ideally, use Summoners :Summoners: with Ginseng Pet :Ginseng:

  • Summoners :Summoners:: More AOE damage
  • Ginseng Pet :Ginseng:: There's a 100% chance of leeching 150 HP from the enemy

Archers can use: :RedBow: for HP Steal

  • There's a 10% chance of leeching 150 HP from the enemy

Use :PetDoll: and :PartnerDoll: for maximum DPS

  • Everyone does the same damage: Players = Pets

Remove HP bonus equipment & jewelry

  • Percent % Damage Raid: More HP = Receive more damage

If the team has fewer than 12 members, only a few :Melee: should be on guard preferably Marus :Marus: with AOE pets

Prioritize Distance & Speed for better positioning and survival.

Strategy

1 - Straight out of the gate, fast as you can

2 - Send pets with F key to the right

  • This constitutes the PET ZONE

3 - Simultaneously Run yourself to the left

  • :SP3A: Surround Queen, plant :Bomb: when you first cross map & clear the area
  • In case of less than 4 :SP3A: : plant bombs near pet zone – not too much, don't risk them killed

4 - Fire! Pets on attack mode with A Key at the Queen

5 - As soon as 🟢 Eggs Spawn, :SP3A: Detonate :Bomb: ! and Launch Mines

6 - Repeat :Bomb: planting after each 🟡 Jump

Beware

PET ZONE

Nobody shall enter here!

  • King jumps on players ONLY
  • No players = no pets deaths = no dolls wasted
  • Constant attackers with each equivalent damage to a player

Run to the left

  • NO ONE ATTACKS YET – Neither players nor pets! Till all :Bomb: are planted

Whenever :ChickenEgg: spawn, forget the queen !!!

Always kill BIG eggs first :BigChicken: and the small eggs last :SmallChicken:

Do not forget to AOE pet zone too
