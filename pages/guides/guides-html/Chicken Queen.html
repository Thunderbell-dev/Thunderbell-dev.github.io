        & Constant attackers with each equivalent damage to a player

    • SP3A Surround Queen, plant <:Bomb:1232996289189249024> when you first cross map & clear the area

  • 4· As soon as 🟢 Eggs Spawn, SP3A Detonate <:Bomb:1232996289189249024> ! & Launch Mines
  • 5· Repeat <:Bomb:1232996289189249024> planting after each 🟡 Jump
