## ⚔️The History of the Ritterorden (Thunderbell's Perspective)🏰  

It all began with a small idea 💡. I decided to restructure my existing Discord server.  
At the time of its creation, I was deeply interested in the medieval era 🏹, so it quickly became clear what theme the family should have – the "Ritterorden" (Knight Order) 🛡️.  

My goal was to create a community for German players on an international server – a home where they could feel comfortable 🤗. Our growth was slow but steady 📈.  

However, due to the varying leveling speeds within the family, some members had to wait a long time ⏳ for suitable level partners. Unfortunately, this led to the family gradually becoming inactive 💤 and eventually almost ceasing to exist.  

## 🏰The Current State of the Family⚔️  

Today, the family is largely empty 🏜️.  

At the beginning of the year, some of my friends started playing Nostale 🎮, and I invited them into the family. A former member recognized the familiar name 👀 and returned. Additionally, I am keeping the family alive with my own secondary characters 👥.

For now, the family remains on hold 🛑 and will not be reactivated in the near future. However, I will keep it existing as a fallback for various situations 🔄.

## 🎭[Fun Fact] Character Name Origins🌟  

My main character is named "Todes_Ritter" (Death Knight 💀⚔️), fitting the theme of the family. However, to create a guild, at least two additional characters are required – and thus, my "main sub-characters" were born:

⚡ Thunderbell 🔔 – The name was a deliberate choice based on the qualities I expect from an archer

It should be as fast as lightning ⚡ (Thunder) and its damage should ring in the enemies' ears like a bell 🔔 (Bell). Even though my character hasn't fully reached this goal yet, I'm working on it! 💪

❄️_Frozenheart_ ❤️ – The inspiration for this name came from the Game League of Legends 🎮. "Frozen Heart" is an item in the game that is perfect for tanks 🛡️.  

I found the name ideal: "Frozen" symbolizes the awe of enemies when they see the mighty tank ❄️, and "Heart" represents the big heart ❤️ of a protector, made to defend his loved ones. 🛡️💖

✨ Sparkwhisper 🌬️ – This name was created by softening "Thunderbell."  

"Thunder" became "Spark" (a small but powerful flash of energy ⚡), and "Bell" turned into "Whisper" (a soft but persistent presence 🌬️). This results in a name that sounds gentler but still retains the essence of its origin! 🎇
